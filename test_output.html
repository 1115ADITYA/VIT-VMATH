Educational Breakdown

A Continuous Uniform Distribution over the interval [a, b] means that all outcomes in this interval are equally likely. Here we have a = 0 and b = 10.
Step 1: Calculate Probability Density Function (PDF)
The PDF $f(x)$ is constant for all values within $[a, b]$, creating a rectangle with total area 1.
$$ f(x) = \frac{1}{b - a} $$ $$ f(x) = \frac{1}{10 - 0} = \frac{1}{10} \approx 0.1000 $$
Step 2: Calculate the Mean (Expected Value)
The mean $\mu$ is simply the exact center (midpoint) of the interval.
$$ \mu = \frac{a + b}{2} $$ $$ \mu = \frac{0 + 10}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5 $$
Step 3: Calculate Variance
The variance $\sigma^2$ measures how spread out the distribution is.
$$ \sigma^2 = \frac{(b - a)^2}{12} $$ $$ \sigma^2 = \frac{(10 - 0)^2}{12} = \frac{100}{12} \approx 8.3333 $$
Step 4: Calculate Standard Deviation
The standard deviation $\sigma$ is the square root of the variance.
$$ \sigma = \sqrt{\sigma^2} $$ $$ \sigma = \sqrt{8.3333} \approx 2.8868 $$
Step 5: Calculate Probability P(X ≤ 5)
The probability corresponds to the area of the rectangle covering the selected range.
$$ F(x) = \frac{x - a}{b - a} $$ $$ = \frac{5 - 0}{10 - 0} $$ $$ = 0.5000 $$
